How It Works

📸

1. List Your Product

Upload a photo, set a price and describe your item, then submit for admin review.

Our admin checks every listing to ensure it's suitable for the student marketplace.

🏠

4. Get It Delivered

The buyer's student ID and campus address are shared with the seller for delivery.
